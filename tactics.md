Tactics
The strength bonus should be dependent on the training of the tactic of the team that might receive the bonus. 

Available tactics: 
Normal 
Pressing
Counter attack
Through the middle
Over the flank
OneTouch 
Kick and Rush


Examples: 
Team 1: Team strength: 1000; chosen tactic: normal
Team 2: Team strength: 1500; chosen tactic: pressing
Team 1 will receive a bonus of 10%*the percentage the team has trained the tactic "normal". If the training of normal for team 1 is 100% then they get a 10% bonus on the strength, that means 100 bonus and a result strength of 1100. For Team 2 nothing changes. 

The bonus is 10% for one tactic below, 5% for two tactics below and 2.5% for three tactics below. If both teams chose the same tactic there is no tactical bonus.
