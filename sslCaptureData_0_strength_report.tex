\section*{How Formulas Are Obtained}
For each candidate feature set, the script builds a design matrix $X$ and solves the normal equation
$$\beta = (X^T X)^{-1} X^T y$$
with Gaussian elimination on $X^TX$. Predictions are $\hat{y}=X\beta$. Models are ranked by adjusted $R^2$ (primary) and $R^2$ (secondary).
Per-position ranking uses compact models (max 4 columns) to reduce overfitting and then aggregates ranks across positions.
